A Memory Game That Grows an Orchard

Orchrd is a free iPhone memory-matching game. Pick the right fruit, earn coins, and spend them on new trees and upgrades to grow your own orchard. No ads and no account.

Features

🍎 Memory Matching

Watch the fruit, remember where it is, and pick it before time runs out

🌳 Grow Your Orchard

Spend your coins on new trees and upgrades

🚫 No Ads

Free to play, with no ads and no account

🔒 Privacy First

Your progress stays on your device. No analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sent to us.
