Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

This policy applies to Orchrd - Orchard Match ("Orchrd"), a free iPhone game.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Orchrd is designed to collect as little as possible. It is free to download, has no ads, and has no account or sign-in. Your game progress belongs to you and stays on your device.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, and no personal information collected
  • Game progress is saved only on your device
  • No ads and no advertising identifier
  • No analytics, no usage tracking, and no third-party services
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else
  • No data is sold or shared for advertising

Game Progress and Data Storage

Your orchard, coins, trees, settings, and progress are saved only on your device. We do not receive a copy and we do not store them on any server. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

In-App Purchase

Orchrd offers one optional, one-time $4.99 in-app purchase called "Orchrd Supporter," which adds a cosmetic founder ribbon to your orchard. It does not change gameplay.

The purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID. We only learn from Apple, on your device, that the purchase was made so the ribbon can be shown. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

No Analytics

Orchrd does not collect personal data and does not collect analytics. It has no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not send information about you or how you play to the developer or to any third party.

No Advertising or Sale of Data

Orchrd has no ads. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising purposes.

Permissions

Orchrd does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal data on your device.

Children's Privacy

Orchrd is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and it contains no ads, accounts, or social features.

Data Deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you control it. Deleting the app permanently removes it. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the App Store release notes for the update.
