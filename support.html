About Orchrd

Orchrd - Orchard Match is a free memory-matching game for iPhone. Watch the fruit, remember where it is, pick it to earn coins, and spend your coins on trees and upgrades for your orchard.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Orchrd Supporter purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Restoring works on a new phone or after reinstalling the app, and you are never charged again.

What does Orchrd Supporter do?

It is an optional, one-time $4.99 purchase that adds a cosmetic founder ribbon to your orchard. It does not add coins, trees, or time, so the game plays the same either way. Payment is handled entirely by Apple.

Where is my game progress saved?

Only on your device. Orchrd has no account and no cloud save, so we cannot see or restore your progress. Your progress may be included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), so restoring your phone from a backup restores it too.

How do I reset my game and start over?

Delete Orchrd from your iPhone and install it again from the App Store. This permanently erases your saved progress on that device. If you bought Orchrd Supporter, use Restore Purchases afterwards to bring the ribbon back.

How do I turn off analytics?

Go to Settings → Anonymous analytics in the app and switch it off. The analytics are anonymous and never include personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does Orchrd have ads?

No. Orchrd has no ads and does not require an account.

How do I report a bug?

Please email gregaltonios@gmail.com and include:

  • What happened and what you expected to happen
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The Orchrd version (shown below)
  • A screenshot, if you can

Troubleshooting

No sound

  • Check your iPhone volume and that the Silent switch is off
  • Make sure sound and music are turned on in the app's Settings
  • Force quit Orchrd and reopen it

App crashes or freezes

  • Force quit the app and reopen it
  • Restart your iPhone
  • Make sure you are running the latest version of iOS and the latest version of Orchrd from the App Store
